# Warranty Policy — TechGadgets Inc

## Standard Warranty

- All products include 1-year limited warranty from date of purchase.
- Covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use.
- Does NOT cover: physical damage, water damage (unless IP68 rated), unauthorized modifications, cosmetic wear.

## Extended Warranty (TechCare+)

- Available for purchase within 30 days of product purchase.
- Extends coverage to 3 years total.
- Includes accidental damage protection (2 claims per year, $49 deductible).
- Covers battery replacement after 80% capacity degradation.

## Warranty Claim Process

1. Customer provides proof of purchase (order number or receipt).
2. Agent verifies warranty status in system.
3. Customer describes the issue.
4. If eligible: issue RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) number.
5. Customer ships item using prepaid label.
6. Replacement shipped within 5 business days of receiving defective item.

## Non-Warranty Repairs

- Out-of-warranty repairs available at authorized service centers.
- Diagnostic fee: $29.99 (waived if repair is completed).
- Repair costs vary by product and issue.

## Escalation

- Warranty disputes: escalate to warranty claims department.
- Claims over $300: require supervisor approval.
- Customers threatening legal action: escalate to legal team immediately, do not make commitments.
