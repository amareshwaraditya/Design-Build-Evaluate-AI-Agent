# Refund Policy — TechGadgets Inc

## Standard Refund Policy

- Products may be returned within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
- Items must be in original packaging, unused, and with receipt/order confirmation.
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after receiving the returned item.

## Digital Products

- Digital downloads and software licenses are non-refundable after activation.
- Exception: If the product is defective or not as described, a refund may be issued within 14 days.

## Exceptions

- Sale items are final sale and cannot be returned unless defective.
- Gift cards are non-refundable.
- Custom/personalized items cannot be returned unless there is a manufacturing defect.

## Damaged or Defective Items

- Report within 48 hours of delivery with photos of damage.
- We will arrange a free return shipping label.
- Replacement or full refund will be issued within 3 business days of receiving the item.

## Late Returns (31-60 days)

- Store credit only (no cash refund).
- Item must be unused and in original packaging.
- 15% restocking fee applies.

## Escalation

- If a customer is unsatisfied with the refund decision, escalate to a senior support agent.
- Cases involving amounts over $500 require manager approval.
