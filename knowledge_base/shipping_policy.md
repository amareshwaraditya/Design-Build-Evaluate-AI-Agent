# Shipping Policy — TechGadgets Inc

## Shipping Options

- **Standard Shipping**: 5-7 business days, free for orders over $50.
- **Express Shipping**: 2-3 business days, $9.99.
- **Next-Day Shipping**: 1 business day, $19.99 (order before 2 PM EST).

## Tracking

- All orders include tracking. Tracking number sent via email within 24 hours of shipment.
- Track via our website or the carrier's website (FedEx, UPS, USPS).

## International Shipping

- Available to select countries. Delivery in 10-21 business days.
- Customer is responsible for customs duties and import taxes.
- International orders are non-returnable unless defective.

## Lost or Delayed Packages

- If tracking shows "delivered" but package not received: wait 48 hours, check with neighbors, then contact us.
- If package is lost in transit (no update for 7+ days): we will file a claim and either reship or refund.
- Weather or carrier delays are not within our control; we cannot guarantee delivery dates during peak seasons.

## Address Changes

- Address changes are possible only before the order is shipped.
- Once shipped, the customer must contact the carrier directly for rerouting (fees may apply).

## Escalation

- Packages lost for more than 14 days: escalate to logistics team.
- High-value shipments (over $200) with issues: escalate to senior support.
