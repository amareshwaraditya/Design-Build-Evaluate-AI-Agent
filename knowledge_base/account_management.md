# Account Management — TechGadgets Inc

## Password Reset

1. Go to techgadgets.com/forgot-password.
2. Enter registered email address.
3. Check email (including spam folder) for reset link.
4. Link expires in 1 hour.
5. If email not received after 15 minutes: verify correct email was used, or contact support.

## Account Deletion

- Customers can request account deletion via Settings > Account > Delete Account.
- Deletion is permanent and irreversible after 30-day grace period.
- Active subscriptions must be cancelled before deletion.
- Order history and warranty claims are retained for legal compliance (anonymized).

## Subscription Management

- Subscriptions can be managed at techgadgets.com/subscriptions.
- Cancel anytime — access continues until end of billing period.
- No partial refunds for mid-cycle cancellations.
- Downgrade takes effect at next billing cycle.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

- Strongly recommended for all accounts.
- Setup: Settings > Security > Enable 2FA.
- Supported methods: Authenticator app (recommended), SMS.
- If locked out of 2FA: contact support with identity verification (order history + registered email).

## Data Privacy

- We do not sell customer data.
- Data export available: Settings > Privacy > Download My Data.
- GDPR/CCPA requests: processed within 30 days.
- Support agents cannot access full payment details (only last 4 digits).

## Escalation

- Account security concerns (suspected breach): immediate escalation to security team.
- Legal/compliance requests: escalate to legal department.
- Support agents MUST NOT provide account access without identity verification.
